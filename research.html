Journal publications

2026

Institutionalised Barriers to Digital Ability in India: An Empirical Examination ↗

Indian Journal of Human Development · Online First
With P. C. Mohanty

Research summary

Using nationally representative household survey data, this study examines how socioeconomic and institutional factors shape digital ability in India. It finds that school type and language of instruction are important barriers, with implications for more inclusive digital education.

2024

Caste and class interactions in inequality in access to sanitation and hygiene services in India ↗

Journal of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene for Development · 14(5)
With Suman Kharb

Research summary

This study examines how caste and class intersect in access to sanitation and hygiene services. Nationally representative household data reveal substantial disparities, highlighting the need for policies that address social and economic disadvantage together.

2023

Undernutrition and anaemia among Indian adolescents: role of dietary diversity and hygiene practices ↗

Journal of Nutritional Science · 12, e33
With P. C. Mohanty

Research summary

Drawing on the Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey, this paper investigates undernutrition and anaemia among adolescents in India. Dietary diversity, hygienic practices, and household socioeconomic conditions emerge as important factors in adolescent nutritional outcomes.

2022

Does maternal overnutrition carry child undernutrition in India? ↗

PLOS ONE · 17(6)
With P. C. Mohanty

Research summary

Using National Family Health Survey data, this paper studies the coexistence of maternal overweight or obesity and child undernutrition. It examines the household and individual factors associated with this double burden of malnutrition and discusses implications for nutrition policy.

2022

Decoding the Three Pandemic Budgets: Education, Health, and Nutrition ↗

Economic and Political Weekly · 57(21)
With P. C. Mohanty

Research summary

An examination of social-sector priorities across three pandemic budgets, focusing on education, health, and nutrition. The commentary argues for greater attention to human development in public expenditure.

2021

COVID-19: A Prospective Analysis of the Implications of School Closure on Children’s Education and Nutrition ↗

CHILDREN FIRST Journal on Children’s Lives · 1(1)
With P. C. Mohanty

Research summary

This paper considers how school closures and the shift to online education may widen educational and nutritional inequality. It examines digital access alongside children’s reliance on school meals and nutrition programmes.
