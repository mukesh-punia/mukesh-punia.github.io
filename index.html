About me

My research lies primarily in applied development and health economics. I am particularly interested in nutrition, socioeconomic inequality, gender, and water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH). My work examines the early origins and life-course evolution of health and human capital.

I investigate how childhood nutrition relates to later health, cognitive development, and well-being, and how socioeconomic inequalities shape access to maternal and child health and nutrition programmes. Through this work, I aim to understand how public policy and investment can support health and human development.

I obtained my PhD in Economics from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in December 2025, supervised by Prof. P. C. Mohanty. Before joining Pahlé India Foundation, I was a Research Associate at the VII State Finance Commission, Haryana.
